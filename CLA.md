# Individual contributor licence agreement

Draft version 1, 2026-09-27.

This agreement is between you, the individual contributor, and Maxwell Industries LLC (the company). It covers contributions you intentionally submit for inclusion in the Ducky game or its documentation, including code, bug fixes, levels and creative assets. It covers your present and future contributions under this version. Work clearly marked as not offered for contribution is excluded.

## 1. Your copyright

You keep copyright in your contributions. You may use and license your own work elsewhere, subject to any rights in company or third-party material it incorporates. The licence below does not transfer ownership. An asset assignment requires the separate choice in section 7.

## 2. Copyright licence

You grant the company a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, no-charge and irrevocable copyright licence to use, reproduce, modify, make derivative works from, publicly display, publicly perform and distribute your contributions and those derivative works.

The company may sublicense and relicense those rights to others, under open source or proprietary terms, for commercial or noncommercial use. This includes licensing contributed code under MIT and licensing contributed assets under the company's asset terms. No further consent or payment is required for these uses. Recipients receive rights under the terms the company grants them.

## 3. Patent licence

You grant the company and recipients of its distributions a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free and no-charge patent licence to make, have made, use, offer for sale, sell, import and otherwise transfer your contributions and the project to which you contributed.

This grant covers only patent claims you can license that are necessarily infringed by your contribution alone or by its combination with the project as submitted. It does not cover unrelated patents. The grant is irrevocable except that a recipient's patent licence for the affected contribution or project ends if that recipient files patent litigation, including a cross-claim or counterclaim, alleging that the contribution or project infringes a patent.

## 4. Your right to contribute

You confirm that you have the legal capacity and authority to enter this agreement and grant these rights. You created the work or have permission from every relevant rights holder to submit it on these terms. If an employer or another party has rights in it, you have their permission or a waiver sufficient for these grants.

Identify any third-party work separately, with its source, licence and known restrictions, before it is accepted. Do not present that work as your own. Tell the company promptly if you learn that any statement you made about your rights is inaccurate.

## 5. No support obligation

You do not have to provide maintenance or support. Except for the statements about your authority and rights above, and to the extent the law allows, you provide your contribution as is, without warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose or non-infringement. The company does not have to accept or use a contribution.

## 6. Signing and records

After counsel review, sign by posting this exact comment on your pull request using your own GitHub account:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

By posting it, you agree to this version for your present and future contributions. Sign once per agreement version. Later material changes require fresh consent and do not retroactively change this agreement.

The public signature record uses your GitHub handle, account ID, comment ID, pull request number, repository ID and timestamp. Use a handle that does not reveal a real person's name. Do not add a legal name, personal email address or other private details to the public record. Any identity verification needed by the company must happen privately through a confirmed company contact.

## 7. Optional assignment of contributed assets

You may choose to assign copyright in specifically identified assets you own to the company instead of retaining it. This is optional and is never selected by the standard signing comment.

An assignment takes effect only in a separate written document signed by you and accepted by the company after counsel review. That document must identify the assets, the rights transferred, the effective date and any agreed consideration or retained rights. It must expressly state that you assign copyright in those assets to Maxwell Industries LLC. It cannot transfer company or third-party rights you do not own. Keep that document and personal details out of the public repository.

Until a separate assignment takes effect, you keep copyright and the licence in section 2 applies.
